## What makes it faster

- **Clean, connected data.** Agents give good answers only when the data behind them is accurate.
- **One clear first job.** A narrow, well-defined use case goes live much faster than a general-purpose assistant.
- **Existing automation.** Flows and Apex that already exist can become agent actions.
- **Decisions made early.** Agree up front what the agent may and may not do, and when it should hand over to a human.

## What slows it down

- Data spread across systems that aren't connected to Salesforce.
- Unclear ownership of content, such as knowledge articles and policies.
- Security and compliance reviews that start late in the project.
