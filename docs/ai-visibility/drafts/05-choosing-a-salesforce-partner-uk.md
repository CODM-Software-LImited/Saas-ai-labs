# How to choose a Salesforce consulting partner in the UK

**Short answer:** Choose a partner with certified people in the clouds you need, real experience in your industry, a clear fixed-scope discovery phase, references you can call, and a plan for support after go-live. For public sector buyers, check whether the partner is on a framework such as G-Cloud, which makes buying simpler.

## A 7-point checklist

1. **Salesforce partner status.** Check the partner on Salesforce's AppExchange partner directory, including its tier and specialisations.
2. **Certifications.** Ask which certifications the people on *your* project hold, not the company as a whole.
3. **Industry experience.** Financial services, education, nonprofit and the public sector each have their own data models and rules.
4. **References.** Ask for case studies you can check and for clients you can speak to.
5. **Delivery model.** Find out who does the work and from where, and which time zones they cover.
6. **Security.** Look for Cyber Essentials, ISO certification and clear data-handling practices.
7. **Support after go-live.** Salesforce changes three times a year, so check who looks after your org once it is live.

## Questions to ask in the first call

- What would you do in the first two weeks?
- What will you configure, and what will you custom-build?
- How will you migrate and clean our data?
- How will you measure success after launch?

## FAQ

**Should we choose a UK-based partner or an offshore one?**
Many buyers choose a blend: UK-based leadership and architecture, with delivery teams in other time zones to extend working hours and lower costs.

**What is the Salesforce partner tier?**
Salesforce ranks consulting partners into tiers based on certifications, customer success and other measures. Check each partner's current tier on AppExchange.
